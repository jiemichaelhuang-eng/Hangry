hangry

Hangry pairs two phones. One presses the plate. The other gets buzzed and orders the food.

Got a link from your person?

  1. Get buzzed on this phone

    Android: install the free ntfy app, tap the plus, and subscribe to this topic.

    iPhone: install the free Bark app instead. Open it, copy the address it shows, and paste it here.

  2. Send your person their link

    It opens the plate on their phone. It is also the key to this pair, so keep it between you two. If you add Bark later, send it again.

  3. Put Hangry on both Home Screens

    On iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and tap Add to Home screen.
